# Bias Detection

Sources carry biases from design choices, funding relationships, publication incentives, and methodological limitations. Systematic bias detection prevents uncritical propagation of flawed conclusions.

## Source-Level Biases

| Bias Type | Mechanism | Detection |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| **Selection bias** | Non-representative sample | Check sampling method, inclusion criteria, demographics |
| **Measurement bias** | Inaccurate or inconsistent instruments | Review measurement tools, calibration, and validation |
| **Confounding** | Uncontrolled variables creating spurious associations | Check for adjustment methods, potential confounders |
| **Publication bias** | Positive results published disproportionately | Look for null results, pre-registration, replication studies |
| **Funding bias** | Results align with funder interests | Check funding disclosure, conflicts of interest |

## Process-Level Biases

| Bias Type | Mechanism | Mitigation |
|-----------|-----------|------------|
| **Confirmation bias** | Seeking evidence supporting existing beliefs | Actively search for disconfirming evidence |
| **Availability bias** | Over-weighting easily accessible sources | Systematic search across multiple databases |
| **Authority bias** | Accepting claims based on prestige | Evaluate evidence independently of source reputation |
| **Recency bias** | Over-weighting recent work | Include foundational and historical sources |

## Detection Procedure

1. **Check declarations**: Review funding sources, conflict of interest statements, and author affiliations
2. **Assess methodology**: Evaluate sampling, measurement, and analysis for systematic errors
3. **Compare across sources**: Note where findings diverge and investigate why
4. **Trace citations**: Verify that cited sources actually support the claims made
5. **Search for counter-evidence**: Actively seek sources that contradict the findings

## Documentation

Record detected biases alongside extracted evidence:

- **Bias type**: Which bias category applies
- **Severity**: How much the bias could affect conclusions (high, moderate, low)
- **Direction**: Which direction the bias pushes results
- **Mitigation**: Whether the source acknowledges and addresses the bias

## AI-Generated Content

When evaluating sources that may contain AI-generated content:

- Cross-check specific claims against cited references
- Verify quotes match original source wording
- Assess whether statistics are consistent and verifiable
- Check for reasoning coherence and logical gaps
